# Legal retention minimums that override deletion

> **Not legal advice.** Sources checked as of 2026-11-12.
> These are **minimums** imposed by other laws. Under GDPR Art. 6(1)(c) and Art. 17(3)(b) they justify keeping *the records the law covers* despite an erasure request – not the whole user profile. Once the minimum ends, delete unless another documented purpose remains. Keep such records in a restricted archive (CNIL "archivage intermédiaire"), not the live DB.
> Periods usually start at the **end of the financial/calendar year**, not the transaction date – check the "start" column.

## Bookkeeping, accounting and tax

| Country | Record type | Minimum | Starts | Legal source (verified) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Finland | Financial statements, annual report, ledgers (kirjanpidot), chart of accounts | **10 years** | end of financial year | Kirjanpitolaki 1336/1997, 2 luku 10 § (1) – [Finlex](https://finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/1997/1336) |
| Finland | Vouchers (tositteet), business correspondence, other accounting material | **6 years** | end of the year in which the financial year ended | KPL 2:10 (2) – [Finlex](https://finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/1997/1336) |
| Germany | Books, inventories, annual financial statements, management reports, organisational docs | **10 years** | end of calendar year | HGB § 257(4) – [gesetze-im-internet.de](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/hgb/__257.html); AO § 147(3) – [link](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ao_1977/__147.html) |
| Germany | Accounting vouchers (Buchungsbelege) | **8 years** (was 10; cut by Bürokratieentlastungsgesetz IV of 23 Oct 2024, BGBl. 2024 I Nr. 323, from 1 Jan 2025 for records whose period had not expired by 31 Dec 2024) | end of calendar year | HGB § 257(4), AO § 147(3) (current text: "acht Jahre"); [BGBl.](https://www.recht.bund.de/bgbl/1/2024/323/regelungstext.pdf?__blob=publicationFile); [BMF 2025 changes](https://www.bundesfinanzministerium.de/Content/DE/Standardartikel/Themen/Steuern/das-aendert-sich-2025.html) |
| Germany | Vouchers at banks, insurers, securities firms | **10 years** (restored by later amendment; already in current HGB text) | end of calendar year | HGB § 257(4) sentence 2; [BMF press release 6 Aug 2025](https://www.bundesfinanzministerium.de/Content/DE/Pressemitteilungen/Finanzpolitik/2025/08/2025-08-06-aufbewahrungsfristen-buchungsbelege.html) |
| Germany | Invoices (VAT) | **8 years** | end of year of issue | UStG § 14b(1) – [link](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ustg_1980/__14b.html) |
| Germany | Commercial/business letters received and copies sent | **6 years** | end of calendar year | HGB § 257(4); AO § 147(3) |
| Germany | Any tax-relevant document | not before the tax assessment period expires | – | AO § 147(3) last sentence |
| Sweden | Räkenskapsinformation (all accounting records incl. vouchers) | **7 years** ("fram till och med det sjunde året") | end of calendar year in which the financial year ended | Bokföringslag 1999:1078, 7 kap. 2 § – [riksdagen.se](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/bokforingslag-19991078_sfs-1999-1078/) |
| UK | Company accounting records – **private** company | **3 years** | date made | Companies Act 2006 s.388(4)(a) – [legislation.gov.uk](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/46/section/388) |
| UK | Company accounting records – **public** company | **6 years** | date made | Companies Act 2006 s.388(4)(b) |
| UK | Company tax (Corporation Tax) records | **6 years** (longer if late return, open enquiry, multi-period items, long-life assets) | end of last financial year they relate to | HMRC – [gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/running-a-limited-company/company-and-accounting-records) |
| France | Accounting books and supporting documents (pièces justificatives, incl. customer/supplier invoices) | **10 years** | close of financial year | Code de commerce L123-22 – summarised by [service-public.gouv.fr F10029](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F10029) and [CNIL](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/les-durees-de-conservation-des-donnees) |
| France | Tax documents (tax-audit right) | **6 years** (10 for undeclared activity) | last operation / document date | Livre des procédures fiscales L102 B – [service-public F10029](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F10029) ⚠ see note |
| France | Commercial contracts and correspondence | **5 years** | – | Code de commerce L110-4 – [service-public F10029](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F10029) |
| France | Electronic consumer contracts ≥ €120 | **10 years** | delivery/performance | Code de la consommation L213-1, D213-1 – same |
| EU (all) | VAT invoices | **set by each Member State** | – | VAT Directive 2006/112/EC Art. 247(1) – [EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32006L0112) |

| Finland | Working-time records (työaikakirjanpito) | until the claim period in Työaikalaki 40–41 § ends (generally 2 years from end of the relevant calendar year / employment) | Työaikalaki 872/2019 – [Finlex](https://finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/2019/872) |
| UK | PAYE payroll records | **3 years** from end of tax year | HMRC – [gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/paye-for-employers/keeping-records) |

## Anti-money-laundering (obliged entities only: banks, payment/crypto providers, accountants, etc.)

| Scope | Record | Minimum / maximum | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| EU (AMLD) | CDD copies (ID documents etc.) and transaction records | **5 years** after end of business relationship / occasional transaction; then **must delete** unless national law provides otherwise; Member States may extend by max **5 more years** | Directive (EU) 2015/849 Art. 40(1) – [EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32015L0849) |
| EU (AMLR) | Regulation (EU) 2024/1624 replaces most AMLD rules from **10 July 2027**; record-keeping in its Art. 77. Not re-verified in this pass. | – | [EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1624/oj/eng) |

Note AMLD is also a **maximum**: keeping KYC data beyond the period without a national legal basis breaches both AMLD and GDPR.

## E-commerce / consumer

| Scope | Rule | Period | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| EU | Seller liable for lack of conformity of goods appearing within **2 years** of delivery (Member States may set longer) | ≥ 2 years | Directive (EU) 2019/771 Art. 10(1) – [EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32019L0771) |
| France | Electronic consumer contracts ≥ €120 must be archived | 10 years | Code de la consommation L213-1 – [service-public F10029](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F10029) |

Order + delivery + customer contact data needed to handle a warranty claim may be retained for the warranty period, as Art. 6(1)(b)/(f) and 17(3)(e) (legal claims). Marketing profiles do **not** inherit this period.

## How the skill should apply this
- Retain only the fields the law covers (e.g. invoice: name, address, VAT ID, line items, amounts) – not login history, IP addresses, preferences or support chats.
- Move them out of the live user record on account deletion (archive table/bucket with restricted access), then purge on the statutory end date.
- Record the legal source per table in the RoPA (Art. 30(1)(f)) and in the privacy notice (Art. 13(2)(a)) with the start trigger.
- If the jurisdiction is not in the table, flag "local minimum unknown – check with accountant/counsel" rather than guessing.
