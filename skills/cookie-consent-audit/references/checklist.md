# Cookie consent audit checklist

> Not legal advice. These checks are evidence-gathering steps; legal conclusions are for counsel.
> Sources checked as of 2026-11-12. Full citations are in [eu-rules.md](eu-rules.md) and [us-rules.md](us-rules.md). Tracker and cookie classification is in [tracker-domains.md](tracker-domains.md).

## Test setup (applies to all checks)
1. Use a **fresh browser profile** (no cookies or storage, no extensions, no ad blocker). Clear site data between runs.
2. Do three runs:
   - **EU run:** an EU IP if possible, GPC **off**.
   - **US run:** a California IP if possible, GPC **off**.
   - **US-GPC run:** a California IP, GPC **on** (`Sec-GPC: 1` header and `navigator.globalPrivacyControl = true`).
3. On each run, capture:
   - cookies (all domains)
   - `localStorage`, `sessionStorage`, IndexedDB
   - every network request (HAR), including image beacons, `fetch`/`sendBeacon`, and iframes

   Capture at three stages: **(a) on load, before any interaction**; **(b) after "Reject all"**; **(c) after "Accept all"** (control).
4. Classify every request and storage entry with [tracker-domains.md](tracker-domains.md). Anything that is not strictly necessary counts as a finding at stages (a) and (b).
5. Screenshot the first and second banner layers at desktop and at 375px mobile width.

## Severity guide
- **Critical:** unlawful processing is happening now, or the user cannot refuse (trackers before consent, no reject, GPC ignored).
- **High:** a choice is materially distorted, or a required mechanism is missing (asymmetric buttons, no withdrawal route, cookie wall, no Do Not Sell link).
- **Medium:** consent may still be invalid but impact is lower (incomplete information, no granularity, over-long lifetime).
- **Low:** best-practice or hygiene gaps (re-prompt interval, documentation).

## Jurisdiction notes
- **EU/EEA visitors:** CC-01 to CC-13 and CC-16 apply (opt-in model).
- **UK visitors:** as for the EU, but the DUAA 2025 statistical and appearance exceptions may cover some first-party analytics, provided there is an opt-out. Do **not** carry this over to EU visitors.
- **US visitors:** CC-13 to CC-16 apply, plus CC-03 in the CPPA §7004 symmetry sense where a banner is shown. Prior consent (CC-01) is generally **not** required in the US except for sensitive data.
